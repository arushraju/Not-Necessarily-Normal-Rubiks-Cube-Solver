Not Necessarily Normal Rubiks Cube Solver

Hello! Welcome to my website of rubiks cube solver. You will have to enter the dimension of rubiks cube you wish to solve. And hit the Enter button. Due to some bugs in the algrithm, the program could help you to solve even dimensioned rubiks cube only. Efforts to make it available for all dimension number is going on and will be avalaible soon.

Now that you have entered the dimension, you will be seeing a grid of squares. It's just the 2D projection of the 3D Rubik's Cube. Click on the sqaures and fill in the color of your rubiks cube configuration (Scrambled). And then hit Submit button at the bottom.
